02 — Heuristic audit

Nielsen's 10, scored against the live screen.

A formal audit of the screen against Jakob Nielsen's ten usability heuristics. Severity assigned by frequency × user impact × disruption to the core task.

5 High 4 Medium 1 Low
H1

Visibility of system status

Banner says 30 fields need attention, but the overview right next to it says 0 fields need review. Two truths on one screen.

High
H2

Match between system and real world

The stepper mixes action labels (Config Product, Config Rules) with status labels (Approved, Published). The number "4" next to "Issues Resolved" is ambiguous.

Medium
H3

User control & freedom

No visible undo for Mark Reviewed. No filter on the 37-item coverage list. No "back to last edited" affordance.

Medium
H4

Consistency & standards

Two parallel navigation systems on one screen: the 5-stage Builder Roadmap on the left, and the 7-step stepper at the top. Both signal "where you are." User splits attention.

High
H5

Error prevention

"Mark Reviewed (0/151)" is a one-click bulk action with no confirmation or preview of what's being marked.

Medium
H6

Recognition over recall

To compare Mini's OPD limit to Medi's, the user must navigate twice and hold both values in memory. Coverages aren't grouped by category. No mental hooks.

High
H7

Flexibility & efficiency

No "next issue" jump. No keyboard shortcuts. No bulk verify. No filter chips to focus on low-confidence fields only.

Medium
H8

Aesthetic & minimalist design

The product structure is rendered three times on one screen — left rail nav, middle Plan Variants cards, right Tree tab. Triple redundancy.

High
H9

Help recognize & recover from errors

Red items appear in the Tree (Age Missing, Family Construct, Pre-existing) but offer no inline explanation of what to do, where to fix it, or why it matters.

High
H10

Help & documentation

No tooltips on metrics. No "what does 89% mean?" affordance. No first-time-user nudge.

Low
03 — The user

Priya — Senior Actuarial Analyst.

Composite persona built from the assignment brief, the existing case-study notes, and standard B2B insurance personas. Her shift is what we're optimising for.

P

Priya Krishnamurthy

Senior Actuarial Analyst · Generali India

Configures 3 health products per quarter. Receives the AI-extracted draft from the underwriting team. Her job is to verify every field against the source BRD, fix what's wrong, and approve before it goes to compliance. Each product gets ~4 hours of her time.

Motivations

Absolute data integrity. A 1% loading error or a misread co-pay bracket can cost millions. Wants speed without sacrificing precision.

Frustrations

Hunting through dense forms. Toggling between Mini and Medi to spot inconsistencies. No clear "what's next" when she's verified ten fields.

Success looks like

"I can see exactly what the AI got right, what it got wrong, and where to look in the source — all in one view."

06 — Proposed solutions

Five moves, anchored to what you already explored.

Each solution is cross-referenced to the actuaria-insight Lovable reference or one of the five "Project Research Overview" variations on your Figma canvas. Nothing here is invented — your sketches converged on these patterns.

01
Problem

Two progress models contradict each other.

The move

Kill the duplicate top stepper. Promote a single honest header chip: 21 to review · 2 critical · 109 verified. The Builder Roadmap on the left rail keeps stage tracking.

Anchored in

Actuaria reference top-left summary chip; your variation #1's header strip.

02
Problem

No prioritised worklist for the 30 "needs attention" fields.

The move

Replace the middle Plan Variants cards with an Extraction Health Heatmap — a 10×N grid of colour-coded cells, one per field. Click a cell → jump straight to the edit form.

Anchored in

The dense heatmap in the actuaria-insight Builder reference (centre of screen).

03
Problem

No cross-plan comparison view.

The move

Below the heatmap, add Coverage Comparison Bars — horizontal bars showing Mini / Medi / Max values on a shared axis. One row per parameter. Mental diffing → visual diffing.

Anchored in

The In-patient / ICU / OPD bar rows in your "Project Research Overview" variation #4.

04
Problem

4-tab right panel forces tab-switching and loses context.

The move

Replace with a single Live Activity feed — Issue · AI Hint · Suggestion · Verified — with filter chips at the top. One scrollable list, no tab switches.

Anchored in

The right-side Activity Feed in actuaria + all 5 of your "Project Research Overview" variations.

05
Problem

No activity history.

The move

Add a "Recent activity" stripe at the bottom of the feed: "Priya verified Mini OPD · 2 min ago" / "AI re-extracted Floater basis · 4h ago". Comments from the existing CommentsLayer can flow into this same feed.

Anchored in

Activity rows visible in your variations #2 and #3; ties into the comments feature already shipped.
